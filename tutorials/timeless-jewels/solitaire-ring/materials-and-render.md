# Materials and Render

### **Step 1: Render and Animation**&#x20;

Go to the **Render** tab and select **Render and Animation**.

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/RenderAndAnimation-Panel-1024x106.png" alt="" height="83" width="800">

### **Step 2: Apply Metal Material**

* In the **Layers** panel, select the metal elements by right-clicking on the **blue layer**.
* Apply **White Gold 18k** as the metal material.

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/White-GOld-1024x342.png" alt="" height="267" width="800">

### **Step 3: Set Gem Material**

* Select the diamond and choose **Diamond material** from the **Gems Materials** section.

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Diamond-1024x269.png" alt="" height="210" width="800">

* For the ground, choose **Basic Reflection Blue** from the **Ground** category.

&#x20;

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/image-54-1.png" alt="" height="400" width="414">

### **Step 4: Rendering Settings**

* Set the **perspective angle** and **distance** to showcase the design best.
* Select your preferred **Resolution** and **Quality** settings.

&#x20;

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Render_animation-1024x452.png" alt="" height="353" width="800">

### **Step 6: Render**

Click the **Render** button to generate the final image.

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Render-1-1024x576.png" alt="" height="450" width="800">
