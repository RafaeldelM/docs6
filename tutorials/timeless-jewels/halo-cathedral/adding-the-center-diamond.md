# Adding the Center Diamond

### **Step 1: Diamond Studio**

Go to the Diamonds tab, then click on **Gems Studio** command.

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Gems-Studio-1024x141.png" alt="" height="110" width="800">

### **Steo 2: Create the Diamond**

Choose a **Round 0.36 ct diamond** and click the **+** button to add it.

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Gem-Studio-0.36-1-1024x355.png" alt="" height="277" width="800">

### **Step 3:  Pose**

Use the **Gumball** tool to move the diamond. Click on the **green arrow** in the front viewport and type **12.0 mm** to place it correctly.

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Move-12-Y-1024x306.png" alt="" height="239" width="800">
