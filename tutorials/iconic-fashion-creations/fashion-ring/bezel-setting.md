# Bezel Setting

### **Step 1:  Bezel Command**

In the "Diamonds" tab, select the Gem and then the "Bezel" command.

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Group-73-1-1024x146.jpg" alt="" height="114" width="800">

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/image-1266-1024x573.jpg" alt="" height="448" width="800">

### **Step 3: Bezel Library**

Choose the BE034 model and click "Edit."

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Group-74-1024x414.jpg" alt="" height="323" width="800">

### **Step 4: Parameters**

Change the height of the bezel to 6.1 millimeters.

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Group-74-1.jpg" alt="" height="500" width="282">
