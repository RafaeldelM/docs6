# Mirror and Classic Shank

### Mirror the assembly

Delete the left half of the cushion basket.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/delete.png)

Mirror the remaining elements along the **Y-axis** to complete the structure.

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/image-2024-11-20T172415.874-1024x1017.png" alt="" width="375">

### Create the shank

Navigate to the **Shanks** tab (1) and use the **Classic** command (2).

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Shank_classic.png)

Click **Edit** and set the **Type: Solid**.

**Important**: Set the Size to **USA 6 1/2**

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/SolidClassic.png)

Advanced:

* **Opening:** Cutting.
* **Cup Bumping:** 1.40.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Cutting.png)

### Adjust settings

Now we will adjust the **height** to match the height of the baskets. Ensure it does not exceed the bridge; this measurement may vary depending on the shape of each basket. In my case, I will use a height of **1.8 mm**. Adjust it to fit your model.

We will also modify the **width**:\
In my case, **3.20 mm**.

![](https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Adjust_widthHeight.png)
