# Place Gem

### **Step 1: Osnap Options**

Ensure the Osnap options "Vertex" and "Mid" are enabled.

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Group-7-2-1024x355.png" alt="" height="147" width="423">

### **Step 2: Move Command**

In the transform tab, selecct the Move command.&#x20;

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Group-6.png" alt="" height="268" width="423">

### **Step 3:  Pose**

Then select the bottom vertex of the gem and move it to the top of the reference curve.

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/image-3-1-246x300.png" alt="" width="563">

### **Step 4: Using the gumball**

Using the gumball, move the gem 2.5 millimeters upwards for proper height.

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/image-4-1.png" alt="" height="224" width="423">
