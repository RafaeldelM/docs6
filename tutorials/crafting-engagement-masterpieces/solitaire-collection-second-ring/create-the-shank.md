# Create the Shank

### **Step 1: Advanced Shank**

Go to the **Shanks** tab and use the **Advanced** command.

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Group-15.png" alt="" height="252" width="677">

### **Step 2: Advanced Library**

Select **element 014,** click on edit.

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Shank-Advanced2-1024x270.png" alt="" height="211" width="800">

### **Step 3: Ring Adjustment**

Ensure the size matches the reference curve by selecting "**United States of America**" and setting the **size to 7**. Change the **Opening** to **8.70 millimeters**.

<img src="https://www.rhinoartisan.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Shank-Edit-1024x345.png" alt="" height="270" width="800">
